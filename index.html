PRIVACY POLICY Magic Jigsaw Pro Last updated: 28 October 2026 NND Gaming Studio ("we", "us", "our") operates the mobile game Magic Jigsaw Pro (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and with whom it is shared. By downloading or using the App, you agree to the practices described here. 1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT 1.1 Information you provide When you open the App for the first time, you are asked for: - Your display name (username) - Your age This information is stored ONLY on your own device. It is never transmitted to us, never uploaded to any server, and cannot be read by us. It is used solely to personalise the App (for example, showing your name on the Profile screen). 1.2 Progress and settings data The App saves your game progress on your device: levels completed, highest level unlocked, and your settings for music, sound, vibration and theme. This data stays on your device. 1.3 Advertising data The App displays advertisements served by Google AdMob. When ads are shown, Google and its advertising partners may collect and process: - Your Google Advertising ID (or a resettable device identifier where advertising ID is unavailable) - IP address and approximate location derived from IP - Device type, model, operating system version, language and screen size - App version, carrier and network information - Ad impressions, clicks and interactions This data is collected by Google, not by us. For details, see Google's Ads Policy and the Google Privacy & Terms page: https://policies.google.com/privacy https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543 1.4 Permissions the App uses - INTERNET — used only to load advertisements. - VIBRATE — used only for optional haptic feedback during gameplay. The App does NOT request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files, precise location, phone number, or any account. 2. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION We use the information for the following purposes only: - To provide the App and let you play both games (Magic Jigsaw and Magic Sort); - To remember your progress and preferences on your device; - To display your chosen name on the Profile screen; - To display, deliver and measure advertising; - To respond to your support requests. We do not use your information for marketing, profiling, or any form of automated decision-making. 3. INFORMATION WE DO NOT COLLECT We do not collect, and the App does not transmit: - Email addresses, phone numbers or postal addresses - Location or GPS data - Contacts, call logs or messages - Photos, videos, audio files or device storage - Account credentials, social media profiles or payment details - Any information for the purpose of selling it to third parties The App has no login, no user accounts, and no server of ours that receives your data. 4. SHARING OF INFORMATION We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. The only third party that receives data through the App is Google (Google AdMob / Google Play services), which delivers and measures advertisements as described in section 1.3. Google may combine data collected through the App with data from other apps or websites to build a personalised advertising profile. If you wish to opt out of personalised advertising, use the "Opt out of Ads Personalisation" option in your device's Google settings or use Google's Ads Settings at https://adssettings.google.com. The App may also contain links to third-party websites or stores. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those third parties. 5. DATA STORAGE AND RETENTION - Name, age and game progress are stored locally on your device and remain there until you delete the App or clear its data. - We have no ability to access or retrieve your local data. - When you uninstall the App, all locally stored data is permanently removed. - Data collected by Google through its SDK is retained by Google according to Google's own retention policy. 6. If you are a parent or guardian and you believe your child has provided us with personal information through the App, contact us at the address in section 10 and we will delete it. Because the age prompt asks for your age, if you are under the age required in your country to consent to data processing, please use the App with the supervision of a parent or guardian. If we become aware that we have received personal information from a child in a jurisdiction where parental consent is required and we do not have that consent, we will take steps to remove that information promptly. Advertising within the App is served by Google AdMob. If the App is designated in Google Play as designed for families / child-directed, Google's child-directed advertising settings apply and the app is configured to serve non-personalised, age-appropriate advertising. 7. SECURITY We take reasonable steps to protect your information. Since all user-provided data stays on your own device, the primary risk is the security of the device itself. We recommend keeping your device locked and its operating system up to date. 8.
